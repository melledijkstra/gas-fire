# Investments, Capital & Debt Reference

While your `source` tab handles the day-to-day cash flow of your liquid bank accounts, your Net Worth is largely determined by your long-term assets and liabilities. 

These three tabs—`investments`, `capital`, and `debt`—allow you to manually track your broader financial standing. The totals from these tabs are automatically calculated and pushed to your `dashboard`.

## Investments

The `investments` tab is where you track your portfolio, including stocks, ETFs, cryptocurrencies, and retirement accounts.

- **Broker**: The platform or institution where the asset is held (e.g., De Giro, Coinbase, Vanguard).
- **Item**: The descriptive name of the investment (e.g., Vanguard FTSE All-World UCITS ETF).
- **Ticker**: The stock or crypto symbol (e.g., VWCE, ETH-EUR).
- **Amount**: The number of shares, units, or coins you own.
- **Value**: The current market value of a single unit. *(Note: While some advanced users link this cell to a Google Finance function to fetch live prices, you can also update it manually periodically).*
- **Month ago**: A reference point to track recent performance.
- **Tendency**: A simple visual indicator (▲, ▼, ▬) showing if the asset has gone up or down in value.
- **Total**: Automatically calculated (`Amount` × `Value`).

At the bottom of the table, you will see your **Total Invested** and **ROI** (Return on Investment).

## Capital

The `capital` tab tracks illiquid physical assets of significant value. This isn't for tracking everyday items; it's for assets you could reasonably sell if you needed cash.

- **Item**: The name of the asset (e.g., Car, Laptop, Furniture).
- **Value**: The estimated current resale value of the item. This is the number that is added to your Net Worth.
- **Sold for**: If you eventually sell the item, record the final sale price here.
- **Original Price**: What you originally paid for the item, allowing you to calculate depreciation.

## Debt

The `debt` tab tracks your liabilities. The total here is subtracted from your Net Worth.

- **Debt name**: What the debt is for (e.g., Education Loan, Mortgage, Home Improvement).
- **Amount**: The original total amount borrowed.
- **Amount paid**: How much principal you have paid off so far.
- **Left to pay**: Automatically calculated (`Amount` - `Amount paid`).
- **Debt period (years)**: The total duration of the loan.
- **Debt yearly**: How much you are required to pay annually.
- **Interest**: The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) on the loan.
- **Planned period (years)** & **Planned monthly**: If you are trying to pay off the debt early, you can enter your target timeframe here to see how much you need to pay per month to achieve it.
- **account / IBAN**: The account associated with the loan, if applicable.
