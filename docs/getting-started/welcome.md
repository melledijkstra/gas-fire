# Welcome & First Look

Welcome to the **YMYL (Your Money, Your Life)** financial template!

This spreadsheet has been built after crafting it many hours for personal use. It gives you a complete financial breakdown of every single transaction over your lifetime, categorizing and aggregating your spending effortlessly.

It is created to have an insight into the economic past, present, and future of your finances. To ensure accurate results and deeper insights, you will want to record as much financial data as possible. Every transaction or exchange of value that you have made over your lifetime is valuable to get a complete understanding of your financial situation.

::: info Note
This financial product is highly inspired by the FIRE movement and the book "Your Money or Your Life", hence the similar name.
:::

## 🎨 Color-Coded Legend

Before you dive in, it is crucial to understand the color coding used throughout the spreadsheet:

::: info **🚫 Grey Cells**
Data in grey cells should **never** be touched or edited! They contain complex formulas and logic. The only exception is the `source` tab, where columns with a light green background are allowed to be edited. All other cells are protected, and you will receive a warning if you try to edit them.
:::

## 🔍 Exploring the Dummy Data

The spreadsheet template is pre-populated with dummy data. This is for demonstration purposes and to get an idea of the spreadsheet.

We highly recommend that you **explore the template first** before deleting anything. Play around with the dashboards, look at how the dummy bank accounts in the `dashboard` tab feed into the charts, and inspect how dummy transactions in the `source` tab are categorized.

> [!NOTE]
> Once you feel comfortable with how everything fits together, you can clear out the dummy data. However do this follow the steps in the next page. _(A built-in script to reset the template to a blank state is not yet available but planned for a future update!)_

When you're ready, proceed to [Initial Setup](./initial-setup) to configure your currency and bank accounts.
